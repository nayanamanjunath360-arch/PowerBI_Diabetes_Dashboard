# Diabetes Patient Health Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze patient health data and identify key risk patterns related to diabetes. The dashboard visualizes metrics such as glucose levels, BMI, and age to support data-driven preventive healthcare decisions.

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **66% of patients** classified as high-risk and **25% diabetic**, enabling targeted health interventions.  
- Detected a **positive correlation between BMI and glucose levels**, indicating potential predictors of diabetes.  
- Highlighted patient age distribution and health risk segmentation to assist in clinical decision-making.  

## 🧠 Tools & Techniques
- Power BI Desktop  
- DAX Measures and Calculations  
- Data Modeling and Relationship Building  
- Interactive Slicers and Visual Filters
